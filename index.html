Then and now

Aprende el Past Simple, el pasado de be y vocabulario útil en inglés. Todo explicado en español, con práctica para cada tema. Toca las frases de ejemplo para escucharlas.

Verbos y sus opuestos

Aprende cada pareja junta. Toca una tarjeta para ver el opuesto en inglés y su significado en español.

Borrow ≠ lend. Borrow = pedir prestado (tú recibes). Lend = prestar (tú das).
Can I borrow your pen? / Can you lend me your pen?

Adjetivos de sentimientos

Úsalos con feel, look o was/were.

I felt excited about the trip.
She looked worried.
He was very tired.

Past Simple

Lo usamos para acciones terminadas en el pasado.

Afirmativa

TipoEstructuraEjemplo
RegularSubject + verb + -edI visited Madrid last week.
IrregularSubject + forma pasadaI went to the cinema.

Negativa

Para todos los verbos: didn’t (did not) + infinitivo.

I didn’t buy a car.
He didn’t go to the cinema.
They didn’t play football.
✗ didn’t went   ✗ didn’t bought
✓ didn’t go   ✓ didn’t buy
Después de didn’t, siempre el infinitivo.

Preguntas

Did + sujeto + infinitivo…?  |  Wh- + did + sujeto + infinitivo…?

Did you speak English last year? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
When did she write the letter?
Where did you go last weekend?

Ortografía de los verbos regulares

ReglaEjemplo
La mayoría: + edplay → played · want → wanted
Termina en -e: + darrive → arrived · love → loved
Consonante + y: y → i + edtry → tried · study → studied
CVC de 1 sílaba: dobla + edstop → stopped · plan → planned

Verbos irregulares frecuentes

No tienen regla: hay que memorizarlos. Toca una tarjeta para ver el pasado.

Expresiones de tiempo pasado

last week · last month · last year · yesterday · this morning · ten years ago · in 2017 · in the 1930s · at lunchtime · last night

Van al principio o al final de la frase.

El pasado de be: was / were

PronombreAfirmativaNegativa
I / He / She / Itwaswasn’t (was not)
You / We / Theywereweren’t (were not)
There were a lot of people at the museum.
There weren’t any video games in the 1930s.
Was there a castle in your city?
Where were you last night?
There was + singular · There were + plural.
There was a castle. / There were two castles.
